Only human acoustically measured HRIRs are used here. No simulated HRIRs.
Original donor source files remain unchanged. Derived files are resampled,
zero-padded and selected as recorded in personalization-summary.json and
each derived SOFA History. Donor selection from uncertain photograph ratios
is not a personal acoustic measurement, or a proven localization benefit.
The separately provided reference medoid is not personalized.
